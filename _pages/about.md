I am a master's student in Robotics at the University of Pennsylvania. Previously, I received my B.S. in Computer Science and Mathematics from the University of Chicago.

My research interests lie at the intersection of robotics and artificial intelligence, with a particular focus on **robot learning, reinforcement learning, and sim-to-real transfer**.

I am interested in developing generalizable learning methods that enable robots to acquire intelligent and adaptive behaviors in complex physical environments.

Previously, I conducted research on geometric understanding in generative models at the Toyota Technological Institute at Chicago (TTIC).
